# VSI control-variate support-state Gate

Date frozen: 2026-09-01

Role: development-only capacity and interaction screen

Status: completed no-go after the repaired true-joint `EXP-051`

This protocol mirrors `.research-control/experiments/protocols/EXP-050.md` for
the reader-memory artifact tree. It tests whether exact regular pages should be
selected by the residual of a mergeable N/Z state rather than by exact
attention mass.

The state is trained only on positions 1--72. All comparisons use exposed
positions 73--96, layers 0/13/27, page size 4, 25% exact tokens, width 32, and
shared normalization. Positions 97--120, official selection, and formal roles
remain unread.

The target-visible joint selector is a capacity ceiling. It cannot establish a
deployable router, reader accuracy, latency, or speedup. A valid miss stops this
post-hoc support-state family before any new training.

The Stage-0 pass rule is visual mean/P95/worst at most 0.5%/1%/2%, paired
bootstrap lower-95 risk improvement over independent mass correction at least
25%, active-state ratio at least 3x, 72 complete cells, and all algebraic
validity guards.

Outcome: the best equal-payload arm was mass-support-trained state plus mass
support at 4.019%/7.651%/10.536% visual mean/P95/worst. Joint residual support
reached 4.281%/13.642%/21.254% and worsened risk by 66.536% relative to that
baseline. Positions 97--120 remain unread; no router, task, or latency stage is
authorized.
