# EXP-013: Rank-conditioned support-manifold cost shaping

## Decision question

Can a fixed-budget regular support chosen to minimize the residual of each rank
action reduce the heterogeneous tail cost below 55%--60%, or is EXP-012's
61.33% test-exposed boundary intrinsic to 35% exact support?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-013`
- Candidate: `L-013`
- Gate: `G-013`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81, Layer 14, step 9, conditional branch.
- Validation: `s02_p00_seed20260741`; one-shot test:
  `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles and twelve heads.
- Exact support: contiguous-64 blocks at 35% total density.
- Initial support: 25% pooled-Q/K proxy plus 10% omitted-block exact mass.
- Rank actions: `4, 8, ..., 36`, plus exact dense fallback.
- Composition: expanded and renormalized under one softmax.

## Registered support search

For every sample, tile, head, and rank action, run `gradient_guided_swaps` with
exactly six maximum rounds, six removal candidates, twelve addition candidates,
and minimum relative improvement `1e-7`. The objective is the normalized
post-rank residual energy. Support size must remain fixed, traces must be
monotone non-increasing, and all blocks remain contiguous-64 execution tiles.

Compare against the unswapped initial support at every rank. No alternative
initialization, geometry family, rank weighting, adaptive round count, or
post-outcome search is allowed.

## Cost and allocation

Use the EXP-012 centered shared-anchor model with exact density 0.35, query tile
64, channel width 128, and feature width 144. Approximate actions costing at
least dense are excluded; dense costs 1.0.

For baseline and shaped support separately, compute exact validation Pareto
frontiers under the 2% worst-head guard. Choose the minimum-work allocation
that reaches 1% aggregate, freeze the twelve rank actions, and execute once on
test. As a registered capacity diagnostic, also compute the test-exposed
minimum-work rank allocation for the same support algorithm; label it posterior
and do not use it as transfer evidence.

## Endpoints and guards

Record rank, initial/final support hashes, swap count and objective trace,
validation/test aggregate and worst-head error, mean/max work, dense heads,
posterior test minimum work, improvement over unswapped support, hashes, and
elapsed time. Dense reconstruction tolerance is `1e-4`; all values must be
finite and every work/support/monotonicity guard must pass.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: validation-selected shaped support reaches test 1% / 2% at no more
  than 55% optimistic work.
- `deployment-boundary`: it reaches test 1% / 2% above 55% and no greater than
  60% work.
- `adaptive-rank-boundary`: frozen ranks fail, but the registered test-exposed
  shaped allocation reaches 1% / 2% at no more than 60% work.
- `cost-boundary`: only shaped allocations above 60% and no greater than 65%
  reach 1% / 2%.
- `null`: no shaped allocation through 65% reaches 1% / 2%.
- `adverse`: shaped support is no better than the unswapped baseline at its
  minimum-work validation allocation or violates a guard.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, adverse, pass,
deployment-boundary, adaptive-rank-boundary, cost-boundary, then null.

## Budget and stop rule

- Existing captures only; at most one H200-hour.
- One valid execution and one pre-outcome engineering repair.
- No learned router, basis, Q/K LoRA, rollout, kernel, or measured speed claim.
- Stop on one valid outcome, leakage beyond the registered oracle scope,
  non-finite output, failed support/work/trace guard, or budget exhaustion.
