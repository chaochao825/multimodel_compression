# EXP-008: Positive-residual basis provenance

## Decision question

After a frozen positive rank-64 bulk and 25% regular exact support, can the
remaining AV defect's low-rank output basis be recovered from current Q/K/V
moments rather than held-out defect SVD?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-008`
- Candidate: `L-008`
- Gate: `G-008`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen data and target

- Same four Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81 post-RoPE captures as EXP-007.
- Cell: Layer 14, step 9, conditional branch.
- Calibration: `s00_p00_seed20260740`, `s01_p01_seed20260740`.
- Validation selection: `s02_p00_seed20260741`.
- One-shot test: `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles and twelve heads.
- Frozen checkpoint:
  `results/content_generated_tail_f81_rank64_v1/calibration_frozen.pt`.
- Frozen exact support: 25% contiguous 64-key blocks selected by current pooled
  Q/K only. No dense held-out score, AV, or defect selects support.
- Target defect: dense AV minus the frozen positive-tail output under that
  support and its shared normalization.

## Registered bases

Evaluate ranks 8, 16, and 32.

1. `adaptive_svd`: per-record right singular vectors of the target defect;
   target-exposed capacity ceiling.
2. `calibration_frozen_svd`: per-head basis fitted only from concatenated
   calibration defects; held-out coefficients remain oracle.
3. `v_pca`: top output eigenvectors of current full-token V covariance.
4. `selected_v_pca`: V covariance using only proxy-selected exact tokens.
5. `kv_cross`: right singular vectors of current `K^T V`.
6. `pooled_q_weighted_v_pca`: V covariance weighted by pooled-query Q/K scores.
7. `random_fixed`: fixed seeded orthonormal basis as a negative control.

For each orthonormal basis `B`, the target-exposed coefficient ceiling is
`D B`, and the residual is `D - (D B) B^T`. The basis itself may inspect the
held-out defect only for `adaptive_svd`; `calibration_frozen_svd` uses
calibration defects; all content bases use current Q/K/V only.

Select one of `v_pca`, `selected_v_pca`, `kv_cross`, and
`pooled_q_weighted_v_pca` by validation rank-16 aggregate error, then worst-head
error. Evaluate only that selected content family on test at all three ranks,
along with the positive base, adaptive SVD, calibration-frozen SVD, and random
control.

## Endpoints and outcome map

Record aggregate, worst-record, worst-head, and worst-head-tile output relative
L2; defect energy captured; principal-angle overlap with adaptive SVD; basis
construction MACs; hashes and elapsed time.

- `pass`: adaptive rank 16 reaches 0.5% / 1%, and the validation-selected
  QKV-only rank-16 basis reaches test 1% / 2%.
- `rank-boundary`: adaptive rank 16 reaches 0.5% / 1%, and the selected content
  basis first reaches 1% / 2% at rank 32.
- `basis-generation-boundary`: adaptive rank 16 reaches 0.5% / 1%, but no
  selected content rank through 32 reaches 1% / 2%.
- `adaptive-capacity-boundary`: adaptive rank 32 reaches 1% / 2%, but adaptive
  rank 16 misses 0.5% / 1%.
- `null`: adaptive rank 32 misses 1% / 2%.
- `adverse`: the selected content basis is worse than the uncorrected positive
  base at every registered rank, or any output is non-finite.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, adverse, pass,
rank-boundary, basis-generation-boundary, adaptive-capacity-boundary, then
null.
