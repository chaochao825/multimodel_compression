# EXP-040: Causal temporal marginal-defect subspace transport

## Decision question

After exact sparse-critical recomputation, does the previous denoising step
provide a more transferable rank-64 output-channel basis for the current
marginal `AV` defect than a static same-step calibration basis?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-019`
- Candidate: `L-019`
- Gate: `G-019`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory fresh-identity mechanism screen

## Frozen data and access

- Wan F81, Layer 14, conditional branch, steps 7/8/9/10, fresh two-prompt by
  two-seed factorial identities.
- Calibration: `s00`, `s01`; validation: `s02`; staged test: `s03`.
- Capture only conditional QKV at the registered layer/steps and stop after
  step 10. Tensor payloads must be written under `/opt/data/wangmeiqi`.
- Current 35% support and non-anchor coefficients are target-exposed oracle
  labels. The test tensor may be loaded only if validation passes.

## Frozen candidates

- `previous_defect64`: previous-step defect right basis, no current innovation.
- `previous_defect48_current16`: previous rank 48 plus rank-16 innovation from
  four Q-k-center 16-row current anchors.
- `previous_defect32_current32`: previous rank 32 plus rank-32 current
  innovation under the same rows.
- `two_step_chordal64`: rank-64 causal chordal extrapolation from the two prior
  defect bases at targets 9 and 10, with `previous_defect64` as its registered
  warm-up action at target 8.

All candidates use total rank 64 and oracle current coefficients. Each
previous-plus-innovation candidate is compared with a matched same-step
calibration prior using the identical current rows and rank split. Additional
baselines are adaptive current-defect rank 64, current micro-anchor rank 64,
and previous dense-output rank 64.

## Metrics and selection

- Aggregate relative L2 pools residual/reference energy over target steps,
  M8 groups, and heads.
- Worst record is the maximum head-step relative L2.
- Report per-step quality, principal-angle/chordal overlap, eigengap, relative
  improvement over static same-step basis, and local optimistic work.
- Among candidates satisfying all validation gates, select minimum aggregate
  error, then worst error, then lexical label. Freeze it before staged test
  loading.

## Outcome map

- `strong-temporal-signal`: frozen test reaches 0.5% / 1%, improves at least 20%
  over static, passes at least two of three target steps, and local optimistic
  work is at most 50%.
- `temporal-signal`: frozen test reaches 1% / 2% with the same improvement,
  target-fraction, and work gates.
- `adaptive-capacity-null`: validation adaptive current rank-64 fails 0.5% / 1%.
- `validation-null`: adaptive capacity passes but no validation causal candidate
  reaches 1% / 2%, 20% static improvement, two-thirds targets, and 50% work.
- `transfer-boundary`: validation passes but the frozen test candidate fails any
  registered signal gate.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard precedence.

## Guards

- No post-outcome changes to steps, identities, support, ranks, anchor rows,
  candidate equations, selection, thresholds, or metrics.
- The two-step candidate must use the registered previous-basis warm-up at
  target step 8; no future-step interpolation is permitted.
- Source dense-refresh cost is omitted from local work and must be reported as
  an unresolved amortization cost; no speed claim is permitted.
- A positive outcome authorizes only a new predictor/refresh Gate. It does not
  authorize rollout, kernel, novelty, or deployment claims.
