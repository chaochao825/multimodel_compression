## Operator and controls

- Structured arms use the existing `96 -> 32` nonperiodic 3x3 lifting followed
  by `32 -> 16` pointwise projection, totaling 28,160 weights.
- Motion alignment uses adjacent-frame block NCC on current clean predictions,
  radius 4, latent block size 4, forward/backward consistency, and nonperiodic
  bilinear sampling.
- The unaligned arm has identical weights, features, optimizer, crops, and
  training steps, differing only by identity transport.
- The pointwise control has exactly the same trainable parameter count.
- Target-exposed motion from the final teacher state is diagnostic only.

## Training

- AdamW, 2,000 fixed steps, learning rate 1e-3, no weight decay.
- Random seed 2026081343, four frames and one 16x16 crop per step.
- Final-step checkpoint only; no early stopping or screen-based selection.
- Primary loss is squared finite-jump curvature error weighted by interval
  displacement through the frozen sampling distribution.

  `sha256_file`.

  locally. It does not change any tensor, teacher, arm, identity, threshold, or
  access-order semantics.
- The failed empty capture directory is quarantined under remote `trash/`; the
  repaired capture SHA-256 is recorded in the implementation freeze and
