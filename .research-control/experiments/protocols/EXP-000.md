# EXP-000: Wan denoising-step state-transition screen

- Status: frozen and running
- Date: 2026-08-07
- Gate: G-000
- Claim: C-000
- Candidate line: L-000
- Lane: prove

## Question

Can a calibration-only, low-payload temporal transition predict enough F81
Wan whole-block outputs to justify a separate rollout and H200 kernel Gate?

## Preconditions

- Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B checkpoint, pilot-8 prompts, UniPC 20-step sampler,
  resolution, CFG ordering, and dense attention backend are recorded by hash.
- Synthetic tests verify wrapper restoration, exact Gram reconstruction,
  nonuniform Taylor coefficients, causal fitting, and open-loop recursion.
- No prior result contains the complete F17/F81 block trajectory statistics for
  these eight identities.

## Evidence status and independence

- Evidence tier: prospective representation/transfer screen.
- Independent unit: prompt/seed identity; cell-level values are repeated
  measurements and are not treated as independent samples.
- Calibration identities: prompt indices 0,2,4,6 and seeds
  202608070,202608072,202608074,202608076.
- Held-out identities: prompt indices 1,3,5,7 and seeds
  202608071,202608073,202608075,202608077.
- F81 is primary; F17 is a prespecified resolution-boundary diagnostic.
- Held-out outcomes may be read once by the frozen evaluator. A follow-up cannot
  reuse them as confirmatory evidence.

## Fairness and leakage guards

- All methods use the same dense trajectories, blocks 0/6/12/18/24/29, both CFG
  branches, common target steps 3-19, and full-tensor sufficient statistics.
- Deployment coefficients, ridge scale, stages, and cell certification are fit
  only on calibration identities.
- Certification thresholds are 0.75% aggregate and 1.5% worst on calibration;
  selected cells are then evaluated unchanged on held-out identities.
- `sample_oracle_ar3_innovation` may use a held-out target to fit at most four
  scalar coefficients. It is explicitly transductive and cannot support C-000.
- No method may inspect future residuals at runtime. Current block input and its
  difference from the previous denoising step are available only to the
  innovation variants.
- Report both dense-output-relative and residual-relative L2. The Gate uses
  dense-output-relative L2; no sampled-row metric may substitute for it.

## Method

Capture, for every run/block/branch, exact full-tensor Gram matrices among the
20 block residuals and 20 current-input innovations, plus dense output norms and
fixed sampled rows for parity diagnostics. The model always returns its original
dense output.

Compare:

1. residual reuse;
2. first- and second-order Taylor extrapolation using actual scheduler times;
3. calibration-only per-timestep causal ridge (`l2p_ridge` proxy, not claimed as
   a paper-faithful FFT implementation);
4. three-stage scalar AR(2) and AR(3);
5. the same AR transitions with one scalar current-input innovation term;
6. a per-sample AR(3)+innovation scalar oracle as a function-class upper bound.

Evaluate teacher-forced and anchor-reset open-loop horizons 1, 2, and 3. The
open-loop state recursively uses predicted residuals, while current block inputs
remain from the dense captured trajectory; therefore this is not a model
rollout. Primary screening uses horizon 2, with horizon 1/3 reported.

## Acceptance threshold

`C-000` passes this screen only if a non-oracle stagewise candidate on F81:

- has calibration-selected held-out cell coverage at least 35%;
- has horizon-2 aggregate dense-output-relative L2 at most 1%;
- has horizon-2 worst identity-cell relative L2 at most 2%; and
- uses at most three residual lags and one innovation tensor.

If reuse/Taylor meets the same threshold and the proposed candidate does not
improve aggregate error by 10% relative or coverage by 10 percentage points,
classify `baseline-sufficient`, not support for the proposed contribution.

## Falsifier

- Strong oppose: the per-sample bounded oracle fails the 1%/2% and 35% F81
  horizon-2 threshold.
- Oppose: the oracle passes but every calibration-only method fails and the
  preregistered single boundary refinement also fails.

## Outcome mapping

- Support: all F81 acceptance conditions hold; draft an untouched `EXP-001` for
  rollout quality and real H200 cost.
- Oppose: falsifier holds; park this scalar temporal-transition family.
- Boundary: oracle-only success, F17-only success, horizon-1-only success, or
  held-out coverage between 20% and 35%.
- Baseline-sufficient: simple reuse/Taylor is as good as the proposed candidate.
- Inconclusive: uncertainty intervals or numerical diagnostics prevent a valid
  classification without exceeding the cap.
- Invalid: leakage, incomplete identities/cells, call-order mismatch, corrupted
  Gram matrices, or evaluator changes after outcome exposure.
- Engineering failure: capture/evaluator did not produce valid evidence.

## Resource cap and stop rule

At most two parallel H200 captures, eight identities each, six blocks, both CFG
branches, and 20 denoising steps; at most four aggregate H200-hours. Run the
frozen analysis once. If the oracle passes but calibration-only methods fail,
permit one bounded channel-group or scalar Q/K/V-conditioned innovation analysis
using the same calibration split; it cannot promote without new held-out data.
Stop immediately on oracle failure or protocol invalidation.
