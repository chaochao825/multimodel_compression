# EXP-006: Comparative block-attention sampling mechanisms

## Decision question

Can query-group refinement, no-replacement stratification, or exact partition
separation rescue block attention approximation at equal work, and which peer
mechanism best explains any gain?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-006`
- Candidate: `L-006`
- Gate: `G-006`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen data

- Existing Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81 post-RoPE Q/K/V captures.
- Cell: Layer 14, sampling step 9, conditional branch.
- Evaluation identities only: `s02_p00_seed20260741` and
  `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles per identity.
- Contiguous 64-key blocks; final partial block is padded only for storage.
- Query-group sizes: 64, 16, and 1.
- Work densities: 12.5%, 25%, 37.5%, and 50%.
- Twelve fixed Monte Carlo repetitions for stochastic estimators.

No held-out outcome may change these choices.

## Frozen mechanism proxies

1. `mass_topk_sparse`: exact block mass selects support and unselected mass is
   dropped. This is a target-leaking VSA/Sparge-style sparse ceiling.
2. `joint_topk_cv`: exact joint numerator/denominator correction norm selects
   child-centroid replacements. This is the strongest registered deterministic
   CMAQ/RCAR-style ceiling.
3. `moment_topk_cv`: current Q plus block K/V moments selects replacements
   without dense scores.
4. `heavy_uniform_wr_cv`: moment-selected 12.5% exact blocks plus uniform
   with-replacement residual correction. This is a HyperAttention-style
   heavy-plus-sampling proxy.
5. `joint_pps_wr_cv`: target-leaking group-specific PPS with replacement. This
   is a MagicPIG/LARA-style proposal ceiling, not their actual LSH/random-feature
   implementation.
6. `moment_pps_wr_cv`: deployable-moment group-specific PPS with replacement.
7. `moment_stratified_ht_cv`: moment-score strata plus fixed-work simple random
   sampling without replacement and Horvitz--Thompson correction.

Every approximate `(N_hat, Z_hat)` is evaluated in three registered modes:

- `joint`: `N_hat / Z_hat`;
- `exact_z`: `N_hat / Z`, isolating numerator quality and representing a
  KDEformer/partition-function diagnostic ceiling;
- `exact_n`: `N / Z_hat`, isolating denominator error.

These are mechanism-aligned proxies only. Published end-to-end metrics are not
placed in the same quantitative table.

## Fair-work accounting

- Native arithmetic density counts query-group x key-block products.
- Stochastic methods report requested draws and unique executed blocks.
- For group sizes below 64, also report the union density if all support were
  forced back into a 64x64 block-sparse kernel. This is the hardware inflation
  guard; no MFU or wall-clock value is inferred.
- Full block K/V moments cost is reported separately and not hidden in density.

## Endpoints and outcome map

Primary endpoints are P95 aggregate and P95 worst-record local AV relative L2.
The registered quality gates remain oracle 0.5%/1% and deployable proxy 1%/2%.

- `pass`: a moment-based full-joint method reaches 1%/2% by 37.5% native work,
  while 64x64 union density remains at most 50%.
- `partition-boundary`: an exact-Z variant reaches the oracle gate by 37.5% but
  its corresponding full-joint estimator does not; only a new partition Gate
  may follow.
- `granularity-boundary`: group-1/16 target-leaking full-joint reaches 0.5%/1%
  but group-64 does not, or its 64x64 union exceeds 50%.
- `sampling-boundary`: target-leaking stochastic full-joint reaches 0.5%/1%
  but moment proposals do not.
- `proposal-boundary`: another target-leaking full-joint mechanism reaches
  0.5%/1% but no moment-based mechanism reaches 1%/2%.
- `budget-boundary`: a target-leaking mechanism first reaches 0.5%/1% only
  above 37.5% and by the frozen 50% diagnostic ceiling.
- `null`: no target-leaking joint or exact-Z mechanism reaches 0.5%/1% even at
  50% work.
- `adverse`: a moment-based estimator has a non-finite output, non-positive
  realized denominator, or P95 worst-record error above 25% at 37.5% work.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain the standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, adverse, pass,
partition-boundary, granularity-boundary, sampling-boundary,
proposal-boundary, budget-boundary, then null.

## Budget

One script, one test file, one valid pass, one analysis, and one pre-outcome
repair. No training, new capture, rollout, kernel, or speed claim.
