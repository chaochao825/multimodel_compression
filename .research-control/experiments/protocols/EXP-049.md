# EXP-049: Target-separated conditional rate-distortion frontier

- Status: complete
- Claim: C-028
- Candidate: L-028
- Gate: G-028
- Kind: staged exploratory side probe

## Question

Does a cheap current-state conditional interface create a deployable
suffix-risk versus H200-cost frontier for Wan self-attention, FFN, or whole
block that was hidden by prior whole-block residual fitting?

## Targets and causal interfaces

For target `m` in `{self_attn, ffn, whole_block}` and denoising step `k`, predict
the exact gated module contribution or block residual `y[m,k]` from two exact
historical target states and a current interface drift:

`y_hat[m,k] = a[m] * y[m,k-1] + b[m] * y[m,k-2] + c[m] * delta_h[m,k]`.

- `self_attn`: `h` is the current block input.
- `ffn`: `h` is the exact hidden state after self- and cross-attention, before
  FFN. The exposed screen reconstructs this additive state from saved exact
  contributions; formal capture records the actual interface tensor.
- `whole_block`: `h` is the current block input and `y` is the block residual.

`reuse` and calibration-frozen scalar AR(2) are mandatory controls. The
deployable field uses calibration-only grouped or channelwise coefficients.
The target-visible field fits the same registered coefficient family to the
current target and is only a capacity ceiling.

## Stage 0: exposed local screen

- Reuse the four immutable EXP-003 F81 dense trajectories.
- Freeze p00 as calibration, p01 as policy selection, and p02/p03 as held-out
  screen identities.
- Evaluate all 30 blocks, 20 steps, both CFG branches, and saved sampled rows.
- Report contribution prediction error and induced additive block-output error.
- This stage cannot produce suffix, speed, quality, or deployment claims.
- Promote a target only if at least one registered capacity point reaches local
  aggregate/worst identity-branch relative L2 at most 0.5%/1%, and a
  calibration-only point reaches at most 1%/2% over enough calls to preserve a
  theoretical end-to-end speedup of at least 1.2x.

## Stage 1: fresh suffix intervention

Only promoted targets receive four fresh calibration and four untouched
held-out F17 identities. Capture full-token target histories and exact module
interfaces without changing dense outputs. Freeze coefficients, target/step
eligibility, and fallbacks on calibration.

For each held-out intervention, replace one target output at one registered
layer/step/branch, execute the exact remaining block and denoising suffix, and
measure final latent error relative to the paired dense trajectory. No chained
replacement is allowed in this Gate.

Suffix risk is the energy-weighted final-latent relative L2 from these paired
interventions. Local target error remains diagnostic only. Target-visible
coefficients may use the intervention target but cannot affect the deployable
table.

## Stage 2: H200 cost

Measure one isolated H200 on server 236 with CUDA events after warmup. Charge:

- current-interface construction not already materialized by the exact prefix;
- reading two cached target states;
- coefficient/projection work;
- renderer writes and any fallback/router work.

Measure the exact replaced self-attention, FFN, or whole-block boundary under
the same F17 shape. Report median and p10/p90 over repeated steady-state trials.
No FLOP-only point may appear as an H200 measurement.

For certified skipped runtime fraction `q` and candidate/exact local cost ratio
`rho`, the Amdahl estimate uses the measured target runtime share `f`:

`speedup = 1 / (1 - f*q + f*q*rho)`.

## G-028

A module target passes only if all hold on untouched held-out identities:

- target-visible suffix risk is at most 0.5% aggregate and 1% worst identity;
- calibration-only deployable suffix risk is at most 1% aggregate and 2% worst;
- oracle-gap recovery
  `(R_AR2 - R_deployable) / (R_AR2 - R_target_visible)` is at least 0.70;
- the complete interface plus renderer is measured on an isolated H200;
- calibration-frozen certified coverage and measured cost imply end-to-end
  speedup at least 1.2x;
- neither CFG branch is harmed by more than 10% relative to its AR(2) risk.

Only a full Gate pass authorizes tiny-router training or fused-kernel work.

## Resource and stop rule

Use existing exposed artifacts for Stage 0. Later stages may use one isolated
H200 for at most eight GPU-hours and 35 GiB under a separate EXP-049 root. Stop
on one valid Gate outcome, two integration repairs, split leakage, dense-path
inequivalence, incomplete/nonfinite rows, loss of GPU isolation during timing,
or resource exhaustion. Preserve failed attempts.
