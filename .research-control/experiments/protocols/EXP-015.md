# EXP-015: Dense-anchor residual lifting

## Decision question

Can one legal runtime dense anchor tile recover the EXP-014 rotating residual
subspace, and can an online anchor-only coefficient map lift that correction to
the other seven tiles within the 1% / 2% gate?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-015`
- Candidate: `L-015`
- Gate: `G-015`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Same Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81 Layer-14 step-9 conditional cell and split as
  EXP-014.
- Sixteen deterministic stratified 64-query tiles, partitioned into two fixed
  groups of eight.
- Same 35% initial regular support and shared-softmax semantics as EXP-014.
- One dense anchor tile per group; all heads use the same anchor tile.

## Stage A: anchor-basis transfer

For each group compare:

- `fixed_first`: first tile in the group;
- `q_medoid`: tile whose per-head pooled-Q vectors have maximum mean cosine to
  the group per-head pooled-Q centroid;
- `oracle_best`: target-exposed tile minimizing post-projection residual,
  reported only as a routing ceiling.

Build the rank `[16, 32, 48, 64]` right-singular basis from the anchor residual.
The anchor itself executes dense and has zero output error. For non-anchor rows,
first use target-exposed projection coefficients to test basis transfer. The
Stage-A deployment condition is Q-medoid rank 64 at aggregate/worst-head
relative L2 no greater than 1% / 2%.

## Stage B: legal online coefficient lifting

Fix Q-medoid and rank 64. For anchor rows compute

\[
C_a=D_aB.
\]

Predict non-anchor coefficients using only current Q, sparse outputs, and the
64 anchor `(feature, coefficient)` pairs. Registered families are:

- mean anchor coefficient;
- nearest cosine Q;
- softmax cosine Q at temperatures `0.05, 0.1, 0.2`;
- linear kernel ridge on Q at lambdas `1e-4, 1e-2, 1`;
- linear kernel ridge on normalized `[Q, Y_sparse]` at the same lambdas;
- RBF kernel ridge on Q at temperatures `0.1, 0.2, 0.5` and lambdas
  `1e-4, 1e-2`.

Ridge regularization is multiplied by the mean diagonal of the anchor kernel.
Anchor rows remain exact dense and are not scored through the predictor.
Validation selects the lowest aggregate error among candidates that meet
1% / 2%; tie by worst-head error, family name, then hyperparameters. Freeze the
family and hyperparameters before one test execution.

## Cost

With support density `rho=0.35`, group size `M=8`, rank `r`, and key length `N`:

\[
C_{anchor}=\rho+\frac{1-\rho}{M}.
\]

Q-only similarity plus output lifting adds `r/N`; `[Q,Y_sparse]` ridge adds
`1.5r/N`; mean adds `0.5r/N`. Anchor SVD/solve, support selection, memory,
normalization, launch, and fusion remain omitted and must be disclosed. The
registered maximum optimistic work is 50%.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: the frozen legal lifting candidate reaches test 1% / 2% at no more
  than 50% optimistic work.
- `transfer-boundary`: a legal validation candidate passes but its frozen
  family/hyperparameters fail test.
- `coefficient-boundary`: Q-medoid anchor basis passes Stage A, but no legal
  validation coefficient predictor passes.
- `anchor-router-boundary`: Q-medoid basis fails, but oracle-best anchor passes.
- `basis-null`: even oracle-best rank-64 anchor basis fails.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, pass, transfer-boundary,
coefficient-boundary, anchor-router-boundary, then basis-null.

## Guards and budget

- Anchor choice for the deployment method reads Q only and is shared by heads.
- Non-anchor predictors may not read dense reference, defect, adaptive basis,
  test-selected hyperparameters, or non-anchor coefficients.
- Kernels, solves, predictions, bases, and outputs must be finite. Anchor basis
  projection cannot beat full-group adaptive SVD beyond `1e-6` relative
  tolerance.
- Existing captures only; at most one H200-hour, one valid execution, and one
  pre-outcome engineering repair.
- No rollout, learned adapter, fused kernel, or measured speed claim.
