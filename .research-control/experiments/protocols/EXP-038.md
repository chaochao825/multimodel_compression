# EXP-038: Progressive heterogeneous residual function-class transfer

## Decision question

Can calibration prior plus progressive current-sample residual observations
support a validation-frozen per-head certify-or-dense allocation across one
untouched identity before target-free coefficient prediction is attempted?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-017`
- Candidate: `L-017`
- Gate: `G-017`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope and split

- Same Wan F81 Layer-14 step-9 conditional cell, 16 stratified query tiles,
  M8 grouping, 35% exact support, and query tile size 64 as EXP-014--016.
- Calibration identities: `s00_p00_seed20260740`, `s01_p01_seed20260740`.
- Validation identity: `s02_p00_seed20260741`.
- Test identity: `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Load calibration and validation first. Test tensor contents may be loaded only
  after a validation allocation passes all quality and work gates.

## Frozen action family

- Support remains the inherited target-exposed 35% exact support used by the
  current function-class probe; this Gate does not claim a deployable support
  selector.
- Build a calibration prior output basis independently for each M8 group.
- Current-sample blocks use Q-k-center, 16 rows per block, at most one block per
  parent tile, with nested stages of exactly 4, 6, or 7 blocks.
- Basis candidates are exactly prior/innovation ranks `(32,64)`, `(48,48)`,
  and `(64,32)`, total rank 96.
- Each head chooses one crossed progressive action or dense fallback. Actions
  are shared over all M8 groups and query tiles for that head.
- Non-anchor coefficients are optimal target-defect projections. This is a
  function-class ceiling, not a legal runtime predictor.

## Allocation and metrics

Validation computes per-head residual/reference energy and optimistic work for
all registered actions. Select the minimum mean-work allocation satisfying:

- aggregate output relative L2 at most 1%;
- every approximate head output relative L2 at most 2%;
- mean optimistic work at most `2/3`, equivalent to at least 1.5x arithmetic
  cell speed before omitted overhead.

Tie by aggregate error, worst-head error, dense-head count, then lexical action
tuple. Freeze every head action and evaluate exactly those actions on test.

Selected anchor rows have zero residual. Candidate work uses the EXP-016
support-plus-anchor-plus-rank formula; dense fallback has work 1. Work omits
support generation, gathers, prior/basis construction, projection coefficients,
normalization, memory, launches, fusion, and real kernel effects.

## Outcome map

- `strong-pass`: frozen test reaches 0.5% / 1% within 50% mean work.
- `pass`: frozen test reaches 1% / 2% within two-thirds mean work.
- `transfer-boundary`: validation passes but frozen test fails quality or work.
- `validation-null`: no validation allocation passes.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, strong-pass, pass,
transfer-boundary, then validation-null.

## Guards and budget

- Calibration, validation, and test identities are disjoint; no test action,
  rank, stage, threshold, or fallback may be selected after test loading.
- Test loading is staged and mock-tested. Path resolution/stat metadata is not
  tensor access; test tensor contents require a validation pass.
- Q-k-center selector APIs cannot accept V, dense output, defect, projection
  coefficients, or held-out metrics.
- Sampled-basis projection cannot beat adaptive SVD on identical unselected
  rows beyond `1e-6` relative tolerance.
- Existing captures only; at most one H200-hour, one valid formal execution,
  and one pre-outcome engineering repair.
- No coefficient predictor, online certificate, learned adapter, rollout,
  kernel, or measured speed claim.
- Locked SHA-256 prefixes are config `d3fabe3b3bbaf0d8`, probe
  `470d6e39d1f89501`, core `87c26894d1dcd7e3`, and test
  `50afbd0f0577d34e`; 34 related remote tests passed before execution.
