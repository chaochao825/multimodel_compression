# EXP-009: Signed value-aware latent-pooling ceiling

## Decision question

Can target-exposed signed latent queries over current K and a fixed V sketch
generate a low-rank output correction for 25% sparse-only attention while
retaining a plausible arithmetic path at rank 16 or 32?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-009`
- Candidate: `L-009`
- Gate: `G-009`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen data and target

- Same four Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81 post-RoPE captures as EXP-008.
- Cell: Layer 14, step 9, conditional branch.
- Validation family selection: `s02_p00_seed20260741`.
- One-shot family evaluation: `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles and twelve heads.
- The 25% contiguous-64 Q/K proxy support is identical to EXP-008.
- Sparse-only output uses exact logits and V on selected tokens and normalizes
  over that support. The target defect is dense AV minus this sparse output.
- No support may change. The EXP-008 positive rank-64 bulk is excluded because
  its optimistic arithmetic cost already approaches dense QK+AV.

## Registered function classes

The fixed value sketch is a seeded orthonormal 16-dimensional projection. K
and sketched-V features are standardized per head and capture. Evaluate:

1. `unsigned_kv16_softmax`: one positive softmax pool per basis vector.
2. `signed_k_tanh`: one signed L1-normalized tanh pool over K features.
3. `signed_kv16_tanh`: one signed L1-normalized tanh pool over K plus V-sketch.
4. `signed_kv16_softmaxdiff`: a difference of two positive softmax pools over
   K plus V-sketch.

Use cumulative ranks 8, 16, 24, and 32. At each stage, add eight latent vectors
and jointly refine all active vectors for 120 Adam steps at learning rate 0.03,
with gradient norm clipped to 1.0. Use exactly two seeded restarts. Selection
within an oracle record may retain the lower-error restart per head. Projection
coefficients are always target-defect oracles.

Select one family by validation rank-32 aggregate error, then worst-head error,
then family name. Evaluate only that selected latent family on test, together
with positive-base and adaptive-SVD controls.

## Endpoints and cost accounting

Record aggregate, worst-record, worst-head, and worst-head-tile output relative
L2; captured defect energy; adaptive-subspace overlap; selected restart;
optimization loss; basis construction MACs; exact-support MACs; and a
coefficient-predictor-free arithmetic lower-bound fraction versus dense QK+AV.

The cost fraction is explicitly optimistic and cannot support a speed claim.
Dense work is `2 M N d`; exact support is `2 M N_s d`; one-pool basis work is
`r N (f+d)`; softmax-difference work is `r N (2f+d)`. Unknown coefficient
generation, routing, normalization, launch, and memory costs are excluded.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: selected rank 16 reaches test 1% / 2%, captures at least 90% of the
  adaptive rank-16 defect-energy reduction, and its optimistic total arithmetic
  fraction is at most 2/3 dense.
- `representation-only-boundary`: the same quality and captured-energy gates
  pass first at rank 32, but the optimistic arithmetic fraction exceeds 2/3.
- `rank-boundary`: the same quality and captured-energy gates pass first at
  rank 32 and the optimistic arithmetic fraction is at most 2/3.
- `rank16-generation-boundary`: rank 32 misses absolute quality, but selected
  rank 16 captures at least 90% of adaptive rank-16 captured energy and its
  optimistic arithmetic fraction is at most 2/3.
- `basis-generation-boundary`: selected rank 32 captures at least 90% of
  adaptive rank-32 captured energy but misses 1% / 2%.
- `null`: selected rank 32 captures less than 90% of adaptive rank-32 captured
  energy and misses 1% / 2%.
- `adverse`: every selected rank is no better than the frozen positive base, or
  any validly computed output is non-finite.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, adverse, pass,
representation-only-boundary, rank-boundary, rank16-generation-boundary,
basis-generation-boundary, then null.

## Budget and stop rule

- Existing captures/checkpoint only; at most two H200-hours.
- One valid execution and one pre-outcome engineering repair.
- No new capture, deployable generator, rollout, kernel, or speed claim.
- Stop after the first valid terminal outcome, protocol leakage, non-finite
  output, failed synthetic optimizer guard after one repair, or budget
  exhaustion.
