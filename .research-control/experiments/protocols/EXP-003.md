# EXP-003: Wan full-model risk-certified selective-execution atlas

## Decision question

Can a calibration-frozen, non-chained one-step SA/FFN policy identify enough
safe Wan F81 calls to justify an untouched rollout, without using held-out
features or errors for selection?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-003`
- Candidate: `L-003`
- Gate: `G-003`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Model: Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, F81, 20-step UniPC, CFG 5.0.
- Blocks: all 30; branches: conditional and unconditional.
- Modules: self-attention and FFN. Cross-attention remains dense.
- Methods: one-step reuse and first-order Taylor only.
- Eligible target steps: 5 through 17 inclusive.
- Data: four new prompt/seed identities from the versioned EXP-003 prompt file;
  two calibration and two held-out identities. Prompt text, seeds, split, model,
  config, code, and artifacts are hashed.
- Capture: eight deterministic token rows per block output. Dense model outputs
  are unchanged, and exact full-tensor module decomposition must have zero
  relative error.

## Selection and leakage guards

For each `module x block x branch x step`, select the lower-error calibration
method only when calibration aggregate/worst sampled-output error is at most
0.5%/1.0%. Selection uses no held-out statistic. A reuse action is legal only
when the previous step for that module is dense. A Taylor action is legal only
when the previous two steps are dense. The selector maximizes saved module
work, then minimizes calibration risk, under these history constraints.

The held-out split cannot alter methods, cells, thresholds, eligible steps, or
history constraints. Post-hoc held-out oracles are reported only to separate
function-class capacity from calibration transfer.

## Endpoints

Primary endpoints on held-out identities:

1. selected-call combined block-output aggregate relative L2;
2. worst selected cell/identity relative L2;
3. denoiser work fraction removed using frozen H200 profile shares of 53.88%
   for self-attention and 12.39% for FFN;
4. ideal denoiser Amdahl speedup before runtime overhead.

Secondary diagnostics include module/block/step coverage, calibration-to-heldout
risk shift, chosen-method mix, and a held-out non-chained oracle envelope.

## Outcome mapping

- `pass`: held-out aggregate/worst error at most 1%/2%, removed denoiser work at
  least 16.67%, and no integrity or leakage guard fails. Open one new untouched
  paired rollout protocol; do not claim speed.
- `boundary`: quality passes but removed work is below 16.67%, or the oracle
  passes while the calibration-frozen policy fails. Narrow to certification or
  insufficient-coverage evidence; no rollout.
- `null`: held-out non-chained oracle cannot meet both quality and 16.67% removed
  work. Stop this predictor family.
- `adverse`: the frozen policy violates 2% worst error or materially worsens the
  dense decomposition guard. Stop without threshold relaxation.
- `invalid`: identity overlap, incomplete cells/steps, hash mismatch, nonzero
  exact replay, selection leakage, NaN/Inf, or configuration deviation. Repair
  once without inspecting scientific outcomes, otherwise stop.

## Budget and stop rule

At most four dense F81 captures on two H200 GPUs in two parallel waves, one
frozen analysis, and one pre-outcome engineering retry. No training, kernel,
or rollout is permitted inside EXP-003. Stop immediately after classifying the
gate.
