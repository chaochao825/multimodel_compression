# EXP-054: rCM-on-policy low-precision dense-attention Gate

- Claim: C-032
- Candidate: L-032
- Gate: G-033
- Lane: integrate
- Status: frozen
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Decision question

Does the released four-step rCM trajectory admit a calibration-frozen static
atlas of whole-cell Sage dense self-attention and FA3 BF16 fallback that is
locally transferable, preserves generation quality, and improves the exact
`9.637995s` resident F81 request by at least `1.05x` on one H200?

## Frozen baseline and operator

- Baseline: EXP-052 resident rCM4, request `9.637995s`, denoiser `3.205365s`.
- Model/source/checkpoint, four rCM steps, noise process, prompts, VAE, resident
  UMT5 policy, serialization, BF16 residual path, and output geometry remain
  unchanged unless this protocol explicitly names an attention action.
- Reference self-attention is official FA3 BF16.
- Candidate self-attention is installed SageAttention SM90 per-thread INT8 Q/K,
  `smooth_k=True`, FP8 V/PV, and `pv_accum_dtype="fp32+fp32"` in NHD layout.
- Cross-attention is never modified.
- Every selected cell uses exactly one Sage call. Every unselected cell uses
  exactly one FA3 call. Per-head splitting and dynamic routing are forbidden.

## Identities and leakage boundary

- S0 uses the existing F17 engineering smoke identity only for patch and backend
  correctness.
- S1 uses four new calibration and four new evaluation F81 prompt-seed
  identities frozen in the EXP-054 configuration before capture.
- S2/S3 use four separate final prompts, each with two frozen seeds. They are
  not read during atlas fitting or threshold selection.
- No Sage output, dense output, metric, prompt, or seed from evaluation/final
  identities may alter the atlas, thresholds, backend, or action family.
- Atlas capture stores only scalar error summaries, never reusable Q/K/V or
  dense target tensors.

## Metrics

For output tensors in `[B,S,H,D]`, record relative L2 over all elements, the
worst head, and the worst contiguous 64-query tile. Calibration certifies a cell
only when every calibration identity is at most `0.8% / 1.6% / 1.6%`.
Evaluation allows `1.0% / 2.0% / 2.0%`; a selected cell violating any threshold
on any evaluation identity is a false-safe cell.

The projected resident request uses newly measured full-shape Sage speed `s`:

\[
T_{projected}=T_{052}-D_{052} a c(1-1/s),
\]

where `T_052=9.637995s`, `D_052=3.205365s`, historical self-attention share
`a=0.5388086760`, and `c` is selected-cell coverage. Projection is only an
advance guard; final promotion uses measured complete-request latency.

## Stages

### S0: patch-equivalence and full-shape backend smoke

1. Verify exactly one isolated H200 and the registered source/checkpoint.
2. Patch only each Wan block's instantiated self-attention callable; run the
   wrapper in reference-only mode and require bitwise-equal F17 latent output
   against the unpatched network with the same noise stream.
3. On one real F81-shaped Q/K/V cell, require finite Sage output with identical
   shape/dtype/device and collect interleaved FA3/Sage CUDA-event samples,
   including all conversion performed by the installed Sage call.

Stop if patch equivalence fails, Sage is unavailable, output ownership differs,
or measured Sage speed is not above `1.4x`.

### S1: calibration-frozen rCM4 atlas

Run the eight frozen atlas identities. During every dual call, calculate Sage
and FA3 but return FA3, preserving the exact baseline trajectory. Freeze the
120-cell atlas from calibration only, then evaluate it without modification.

Advance only if all hold:

- zero evaluation false-safe cells;
- selected coverage at least `87/120` (`72.5%`);
- the measured-speed projection is at least `1.05x`;
- all 960 expected cell-identity records are finite and uniquely identified;
- no OOM, backend fallback, missing call, or foreign GPU process occurs.

### S2: on-policy F17 candidate smoke

Run unmodified rCM4 and the frozen-atlas candidate from identical noise. Require
four network calls, complete F17 tensors/videos, finite outputs, paired decoded
SSIM at least `0.98`, and no unregistered action. Record denoiser and complete
request timing, but do not promote from F17 speed.

### S3: final quality and resident F81 timing

Generate baseline and candidate for four final prompts by two seeds. Require:

- paired decoded SSIM mean at least `0.98` and minimum at least `0.97`;
- candidate/rCM mean over the eight registered VBench dimensions at least
  `0.99`, with no dimension below `0.97`;
- inter-seed diversity at least `0.95` of rCM on three of four prompts and no
  prompt below `0.85`;
- four calls, complete 81-frame outputs, finite metrics, and no fallback.

For the timing seed, run the four prompts in the same resident process and
interleave baseline/candidate order. Include positive text encoding, denoising,
VAE, transfer, and MP4 serialization. Promotion requires median complete
request at most `9.179043s`, equivalent to `>=1.05x`, and denoiser speedup at
least `1.15x`.

## Outcome mapping

- `pass`: S1 transfer/materiality, S2 smoke, S3 quality, and measured request
  speed all pass.
- `coverage-null`: S1 has no false-safe cells but covers fewer than 87 cells or
  projects below `1.05x`.
- `transfer-null`: at least one calibration-selected cell is false-safe.
- `quality-boundary`: local atlas passes but S2/S3 quality does not.
- `speed-boundary`: local and generation quality pass but measured request or
  denoiser speed misses its guard.
- `adverse`: candidate is slower, unstable, incomplete, or materially increases
  memory without benefit.
- `invalid`: identity, leakage, source, isolation, backend, or timing boundary
  is violated.
- `engineering-failure`: S0 cannot pass after two bounded repairs without
  changing the registered operator.

## Resource cap and stop rule

- One isolated H200 NVL, at most four GPU-hours and 10 GiB new artifacts.
- At most two bounded engineering repairs.
- Stop on one valid outcome, failed S0/S1 advance guard, isolation loss,
  resource exhaustion, or user interruption.
- Do not add FA3 FP8, head splits, learned routing, cache, sparsity, low rank,
  BCM/BCCB, Butterfly, compile, CUDA Graph, new thresholds, or post-hoc prompt
  selection inside EXP-054.
