# EXP-012: Cost-constrained heterogeneous-rank frontier

## Decision question

Does head heterogeneity permit a validation-frozen adaptive-tail allocation to
meet 1% / 2% local quality below 60% optimistic dense work, or is the rank-32
boundary intrinsically too expensive even with intermediate ranks?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-012`
- Candidate: `L-012`
- Gate: `G-012`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81, Layer 14, step 9, conditional branch.
- Validation: `s02_p00_seed20260741`; one-shot test:
  `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles and twelve heads.
- Base support: pooled-Q/K proxy, contiguous-64 blocks, 25% density.
- Additional expanded-and-renormalized support densities: 0%, 2.5%, 5%, and
  10% of valid keys.
- Frozen selectors inherited from EXP-011 validation: none, atom-energy,
  atom-energy, and mass respectively.
- Adaptive ranks: `0, 4, 8, ..., 44`, plus exact dense fallback.
- This is an outcome-exposed function-class screen on a previously studied
  cell. It carries no population-generalization claim.

## Optimistic work model

For approximate rank `r > 0`, query tile 64, channel width 128, and centered
feature width 144:

```
c(delta, r) = 0.25 + delta
            + ((r + 1) * 144 + r * 128) / (2 * 64 * 128).
```

Rank zero costs `0.25 + delta`; exact dense fallback costs 1.0. Approximate
actions with cost at least dense are dominated and excluded. Report cap
frontiers at 45%, 50%, 55%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 75%, and 100% mean head work.
This model is deliberately optimistic and omits all runtime overhead.

## Allocation rule

For each density, compute the exact Pareto frontier of per-head actions on
validation. Among allocations satisfying the validation worst-head guard,
choose the minimum aggregate residual at each work cap. Also choose the
minimum-work allocation satisfying each registered quality gate. Ties are
resolved by lower work, lower aggregate residual, lower worst-head residual,
lower density, then lexicographic head actions.

Freeze density, support selector, and the twelve head actions before test. The
test identity may use its target-exposed support oracle and adaptive SVD because
the Gate tests only representation and arithmetic necessity; it may not alter
the frozen allocation.

## Endpoints and guards

Record per-head action, rank, validation/test aggregate and worst-head error,
mean and maximum work, exact-support density, Pareto frontier, code/config/data
identity, elapsed time, and all finite/dominance checks. Reconstructed dense
attention must agree within relative L2 `1e-4`. Allocation work may not exceed
its registered cap.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: the validation-selected strict allocation reaches test 0.5% / 1%
  and mean optimistic work at most 60%.
- `deployment-boundary`: the validation-selected deployment allocation reaches
  test 1% / 2% and mean optimistic work at most 60%, but strict does not pass.
- `transfer-boundary`: validation has a qualifying allocation at at most 60%,
  but its frozen test allocation misses 1% / 2%.
- `cost-boundary`: a frozen allocation reaches test 1% / 2%, but its minimum
  validation-selected work is above 60% and no greater than 75%.
- `null`: no registered allocation below 75% work reaches test 1% / 2%.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, pass,
deployment-boundary, transfer-boundary, cost-boundary, then null.

## Budget and stop rule

- Existing captures only; at most one H200-hour.
- One valid execution and one pre-outcome engineering repair.
- No learned basis, router, Q/K LoRA, rollout, kernel, or measured speed claim.
- Stop on one valid outcome, leakage beyond the registered oracle scope,
  non-finite output, failed work/reconstruction guard, or budget exhaustion.
