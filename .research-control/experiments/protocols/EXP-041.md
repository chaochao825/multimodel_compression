# EXP-041: Temporal heterogeneous-rank frontier

## Decision question

Can a validation-frozen per-head mixture of bounded previous-step defect ranks
and at most two dense fallbacks convert EXP-040's temporal-over-static signal
into a prospective transfer pass within 50% local optimistic work?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-020`
- Candidate: `L-020`
- Gate: `G-020`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory adaptive follow-up with an unopened staged test

## Frozen data and access

- Reuse EXP-040 Wan F81 Layer-14 conditional captures at steps 7/8/9/10.
- Calibration: `s00`, `s01`; exposed validation: `s02`; unopened staged test:
  `s03`.
- The analysis must load only calibration and validation first. `s03` may be
  loaded only after the complete validation gate passes.
- Exact 35% support and low-rank projection coefficients remain target-exposed
  oracle labels.

## Frozen action family

- Previous-step marginal-defect right bases at ranks 32, 48, 64, 80, and 96.
- Exact dense fallback, capped at two of twelve heads.
- One action per head, shared across all three target steps and both M8 groups.
- Matched static control: the same frozen ranks and dense fallbacks, replacing
  only the temporal basis with a calibration-only same-target-step basis.
- Adaptive current-step bases at all ranks are diagnostic capacity controls and
  cannot be selected.

## Selection and metrics

- Aggregate relative L2 pools residual and reference energy over heads, targets,
  and M8 groups. Worst error is the maximum head-step relative L2.
- For each head, actions exceeding 2% at any validation target are ineligible.
- Dynamic programming selects minimum mean optimistic work among tables with
  pooled validation error at most 1%, worst head-step at most 2%, at most two
  dense heads, and mean work at most 50%; ties use pooled error, worst error,
  dense count, then lexical actions.
- The selected table must additionally improve at least 20% over its matched
  static table and pass at least two of three target steps before test loading.
- Local optimistic work is `0.35 + rank/key_tokens` for temporal/static actions
  and `1.0` for dense fallback.

## Outcome map

- `strong-temporal-frontier`: frozen test passes all registered gates and reaches
  0.5% aggregate / 1% worst head-step.
- `temporal-frontier`: frozen test passes 1% / 2%, 20% static improvement,
  two-thirds targets, 50% work, and the dense cap.
- `validation-null`: no frozen validation allocation passes every gate; `s03`
  remains unopened.
- `transfer-boundary`: validation passes but the frozen `s03` result fails any
  registered gate.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard precedence.

## Guards

- No post-outcome change to identities, support, ranks, rank granularity,
  fallback cap, action unit, selection, thresholds, metrics, or static control.
- Do not add rank 112/128, current innovation, chordal extrapolation, rotation,
  BCM/BCCB/Butterfly, or new support search in this Gate.
- Source-step dense refresh, SVD/basis construction, target projections, memory,
  routing, launches, fusion, rollout, and measured latency are excluded from the
  cost proxy and must remain explicit limitations.
- A positive outcome authorizes only a separately preregistered target-free
  coefficient and refresh-amortization experiment.
