# EXP-047: Released rCM four-step H200 Pareto Gate

- Status: complete (`speed-boundary`)
- Claim: C-026
- Candidate: L-026
- Gate: G-026
- Kind: prospective exploratory deployment-baseline Gate

## Decision question

On the repository's single-H200 Wan stack, does the official unquantized
four-step rCM Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B release provide a materially faster F81 endpoint
without catastrophic semantic, temporal, or diversity loss relative to the
native 20-step teacher?

This is a released-baseline measurement. It does not validate the rejected
EXP-045/046 state definitions and is not a new algorithmic contribution.

## Frozen methods

1. `teacher20`: unchanged Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, BF16, 20-step UniPC.
2. `native4`: the same unchanged Wan checkpoint and stack at four UniPC steps.
3. `rcm4`: official unquantized `rCM_Wan2.1_T2V_1.3B_480p.pt`, four steps,
   official rCM inference equations, BF16.

The official rCM Git revision and released checkpoint identity are pinned in
the run manifest. Quantized weights, SLA/SageSLA, cache, compile, CUDA Graph,
CFG parallelism, and custom low-precision kernels are disabled so the first
Gate isolates training-based NFE reduction. They may be measured only under a
later system protocol.

## Frozen data

- Model task: T2V 480p, 81 frames, 16:9.
- Formal endpoint: four prompts spanning articulated motion, object
  interaction, camera motion, and fine-detail fluid dynamics.
- Seeds: `2026082701` and `2026082702` for every prompt and every method.
- The prompt text and ordering are frozen in
  `worldfoundry_hybrid_residual/configs/wan_rcm_baseline_prompts_exp047.txt`.
- One separate smoke prompt with seed `2026082700` is engineering-only and
  cannot change the formal configuration.
- EXP-045/046 locked final identities remain unopened.

## Timing and resource accounting

- One NVIDIA H200 NVL; no concurrent foreign GPU process.
- Report cold load, text encoding, denoiser, VAE decode, warm end-to-end,
  per-NFE denoiser time, peak allocated/reserved VRAM, and output serialization.
- One warmup followed by three timed repeats for the smoke; formal generation
  records one end-to-end run per prompt/seed plus a repeated timing subset fixed
  to the first prompt and first seed.
- Model load is reported separately and excluded from warm end-to-end speedup.
- At most 12 H200-hours and 40 GiB under the project-owned `/opt/data` staging
  root. No training or checkpoint mutation is allowed.

## Quality metrics

- Official VBench evaluator implementations on the frozen four-prompt set:
  subject consistency, background consistency,
  temporal flickering, motion smoothness, dynamic degree, aesthetic quality,
  imaging quality, and overall text-video consistency.
- These are teacher-normalized project-set measurements, not a claim of a
  full official VBench benchmark score.
- Teacher-normalized ratios are reported per dimension; scale directions are
  checked against the official evaluator before aggregation.
- Inter-seed diversity is reported from frozen video embeddings and framewise
  pixel/perceptual distances for each prompt.
- Paired frame SSIM and PSNR versus `teacher20` are diagnostics only, not a
  quality Gate, because rCM changes the sampled trajectory.
- Every metric is reported per prompt/seed as well as in aggregate. Missing or
  nonfinite outputs invalidate the scientific Gate.

## G-026

`rcm4` passes the useful-baseline Gate only if all conditions hold:

- warm denoiser speedup is at least `3.5x` and warm end-to-end speedup is at
  least `2.5x` versus `teacher20` on the repeated timing subset;
- the mean teacher-normalized ratio across the eight registered VBench
  dimensions is at least `0.90`, and no registered semantic/temporal dimension
  is below `0.80`;
- inter-seed diversity is at least `0.70` of the teacher value in at least
  three of four prompts and does not collapse below `0.50` on any prompt;
- all eight formal videos complete without fallback, NaN, OOM, or truncation.

`native4` is a required control but cannot pass G-026 on behalf of rCM.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: rCM is the measured deployment incumbent; only then design a
  training-native state whose quality/latency target is explicit.
- `quality-boundary`: speed passes but one or more quality/diversity guards
  fail; retain the Pareto point but do not call it faithful acceleration.
- `speed-boundary`: quality passes but speed does not; profile implementation
  before changing algorithms.
- `null/adverse`: both useful speed and quality guards fail.
- `engineering-failure`: official checkpoint or frozen inference cannot be
  made executable within two bounded environment/integration repairs.
- `invalid`: method identity, prompt/seed parity, metric direction, GPU
  isolation, or timing accounting is violated.

## Stages and stop rule

1. CPU/import and one F17 engineering smoke; no scientific conclusion.
2. One F81 smoke with all three methods and complete timing fields.
3. Formal four-prompt by two-seed generation and frozen quality evaluation.

Stop after one valid G-026 outcome, two failed integration repairs, any identity
or isolation violation, the time/storage cap, or user interruption. Do not add
another released model, attention backend, quantizer, prompt, seed, metric, or
threshold after seeing formal output.
