# EXP-007: Signed numerator tail with separate positive partition

## Decision question

Can a content-generated signed numerator basis close the vector-numerator gap
left by positive linear tails and block sampling while preserving a regular
25% exact support?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-007`
- Candidate: `L-007`
- Gate: `G-007`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen data and split

- Existing Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81 post-RoPE Q/K/V captures only.
- Cell: Layer 14, sampling step 9, conditional branch.
- Calibration identities: `s00_p00_seed20260740` and
  `s01_p01_seed20260740`.
- Architecture-selection identity: `s02_p00_seed20260741`.
- One-shot test identity: `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Three deterministic 64-query tiles per identity and all twelve heads.
- Keys are partitioned into contiguous 64-key blocks; the final partial block
  is masked rather than treated as a semantic periodic boundary.

The test identity may not select a rank, adapter, checkpoint, support, loss,
regularizer, or stopping time. The transductive diagnostic is reported only as
a target-exposed capacity bound and cannot support a transfer claim.

## Frozen support and model family

- Exact support density: 25% of contiguous 64-key blocks for each 64-query tile.
- Support selector: the existing pooled-Q/K proxy from the calibration-frozen
  positive-tail harness. Dense attention outcomes are never used to select
  validation or test support.
- Signed numerator feature ranks: 16, 32, and 64.
- Positive partition feature rank: 32.
- Numerator output-adapter ranks: 0 and 8.
- Total registered architectures: six.
- Original Q, K, V, and Wan model weights remain frozen.

For query row `i`, the approximation is

```
N_hat_i = P(sum_j <phi_N(q_i), psi_N(k_j)> v_j)
          + sum_{j in Omega_i} exp(s_ij) v_j
          - P(sum_{j in Omega_i} <phi_N(q_i), psi_N(k_j)> v_j)

Z_hat_i = sum_j <phi_Z(q_i), psi_Z(k_j)>
          + sum_{j in Omega_i} exp(s_ij)
          - sum_{j in Omega_i} <phi_Z(q_i), psi_Z(k_j)>

Y_hat_i = N_hat_i / Z_hat_i.
```

`phi_N` and `psi_N` may be signed. `phi_Z` and `psi_Z` are positive. `P` is
identity for adapter rank 0 and an identity-initialized rank-8 residual map for
adapter rank 8. Exact interactions replace, rather than add to, each branch's
approximation on `Omega`.

## Frozen optimization

- Optimizer: AdamW, weight decay `1e-4`, gradient clipping at `1.0`.
- Stage 1: 400 steps, learning rate `1e-3`, exact-partition numerator-output
  loss on calibration only.
- Stage 2: 200 steps, learning rate `2e-4`, joint output loss plus registered
  numerator and log-partition auxiliary terms on calibration only.
- Checkpoint selection: lowest calibration objective only.
- Architecture selection: lowest validation joint aggregate relative L2;
  ties resolve by lower validation worst-head error, then lower numerator rank,
  then adapter rank 0.
- Fixed random seeds are derived from architecture names.

After one-shot test evaluation, the selected architecture alone is fitted on
all four captures under the same fixed step counts to measure a transductive
exact-partition capacity ceiling. It is stored and labelled separately.

## Baselines and diagnostics

- Exact 25% support with the unselected tail dropped (`sparse_only`).
- Existing calibration-frozen positive rank-64 tail result, identified in the
  final report as historical and not rerun.
- Signed numerator with exact partition (`exact_z`) to isolate numerator error.
- Exact numerator with learned partition (`exact_n`) to isolate normalization.
- Joint signed numerator and positive partition (`joint`).

Record aggregate, worst-record, worst-head, and worst-query-tile relative L2;
numerator-equivalent error; log-partition RMSE; minimum partition; selected
support density; parameter count; full-sequence arithmetic ratio; identities;
code/config/data hashes; package versions; and elapsed time.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: the selected calibration-frozen model reaches test joint aggregate /
  worst-head at most 1% / 2%, the transductive `exact_z` diagnostic reaches
  0.5% / 1%, all partitions are positive and finite, and the arithmetic ratio
  is at most 0.30.
- `partition-boundary`: transductive `exact_z` reaches 0.5% / 1% and selected
  calibration-frozen test `exact_z` reaches 1% / 2%, but its joint result does
  not.
- `transfer-boundary`: transductive `exact_z` reaches 0.5% / 1%, but selected
  calibration-frozen test `exact_z` misses 1% / 2%.
- `capacity-boundary`: transductive `exact_z` reaches 1% / 2% but misses the
  0.5% / 1% capacity gate.
- `null`: transductive `exact_z` misses 1% / 2% without an adverse or invalid
  condition.
- `adverse`: any evaluated partition is non-positive or non-finite, an output
  is non-finite, or the selected calibration-frozen joint model is worse than
  `sparse_only` on both aggregate and worst-head test error.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, adverse, pass,
partition-boundary, transfer-boundary, capacity-boundary, then null.

## Budget and stop rule

- Reuse existing captures; no new generation or capture.
- At most two H200-hours, six calibration models, one selected transductive
  diagnostic, one valid execution, and one pre-outcome engineering repair.
- Stop immediately on non-finite training, protocol leakage, exhausted budget,
  or the first valid terminal outcome.
- No rollout, VBench, CUDA kernel, or measured speed claim under this Gate.
