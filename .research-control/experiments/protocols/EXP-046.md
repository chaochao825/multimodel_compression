# EXP-046: Full-observability whole-block rank-state capacity

- Status: active
- Claim: C-025
- Candidate: L-025
- Gate: G-025
- Kind: prospective exploratory capacity Gate

## Question

After removing a causal current-input diagonal prediction, does the remaining
Wan whole-block defect admit a cheap target-visible rank-64 state across enough
late layers and over one-, two-, and three-step skips?

## Frozen scope

- Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, F17 480x832, 20-step UniPC, CFG scale 5.0.
- Latent token grid 5x30x52, hidden width 1536, FFN width 8960.
- Blocks 20--29, target sampler steps 4/6, both CFG branches.
- Open-loop horizons 1/2/3 use the same exact-anchor contract as EXP-045.
- Base renderer: the frozen EXP-045 current-input channel-diagonal method,
  fitted only before the exact anchor.
- Target-visible correction: deterministic randomized SVD of
  `exact residual - base prediction` at ranks 8/16/32/64/96, oversampling 16,
  two power iterations, and a fixed seed.

## Identity boundary

- Twelve new prompt-seed identities are frozen before execution.
- Identities 0--3 are calibration-only and initially used only for smoke/testing.
- Identities 4--7 are the one-shot G-025 selection split.
- Identities 8--11 are final and remain unopened in EXP-046.
- EXP-045 identities may explain prior behavior but cannot select EXP-046 ranks,
  thresholds, layers, or numerical settings.

## Metrics

- Full-tensor block-output and residual relative L2.
- Defect energy captured at each rank.
- Aggregate and worst identity/branch error for each layer, step, and horizon.
- Minimum tested rank satisfying 0.5%/1% where one exists.
- State-factor payload and optimistic render MACs `2*N*D*r`.
- Causal diagonal base application MACs and base-plus-render MACs are recorded
  separately. `2*N*D*r` is the frozen conservative state-render proxy; neither
  arithmetic quantity is an H200 latency measurement.
- Estimated state-render arithmetic relative to
  `4*N*D^2 + 2*N*D*FFN + 2*N^2*D`; this is not a latency claim.

## G-025

Rank 64 passes only if, for every target step and horizon:

- at least 6 of 10 layers have aggregate block-output relative L2 at most 0.5%;
- the same passing layers have worst identity/branch error at most 1%; and
- optimistic rank-state render arithmetic is at most 10% of the estimated exact
  Wan block MACs.

No average across horizons may hide an H2/H3 failure. Rank 96 is diagnostic only.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: freeze the capacity result and open a separate current-h coordinate
  observability Gate using calibration/selection only.
- `null`: stop whole-block rank-state observer/transition work at rank 64.
- `boundary`: rank 64 misses but rank 96 passes; do not promote without a new
  cost/architecture decision.
- `invalid`: dense capture mismatch, target leakage into the base renderer,
  split violation, randomized-SVD accuracy failure, or incomplete cells.

## Resource and stop rule

One H200, one calibration smoke, then one four-identity selection execution.
Persist metrics and limited debug rows only. Stop after one valid G-025 result,
any integrity failure, foreign GPU overlap, or resource exhaustion. No state
observer training, rollout, VAE decode, or kernel timing is permitted here.
