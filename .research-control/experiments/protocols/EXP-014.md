# EXP-014: Shared centered-latent tail amortization

## Decision question

Can one content-specific output basis shared across multiple query tiles grow
slowly enough, and can a centered K/V latent family recover it accurately
enough, to cross the 1% / 2% local quality gate below 55% optimistic work?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-014`
- Candidate: `L-014`
- Gate: `G-014`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81, Layer 14, step 9, conditional branch.
- Validation: `s02_p00_seed20260741`; one-shot test:
  `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Twelve heads, 64-query tiles, and deterministic nested tile counts
  `M = [2, 4, 8, 16]` selected without target access.
- Exact support is the EXP-013 initial 25% pooled-Q/K plus 10% exact-mass union,
  expanded and renormalized under one softmax.

## Stage A: shared adaptive ceiling

For every sample, head, and `M`, concatenate the `M` output defects along the
query axis. Evaluate a single adaptive right-singular basis at ranks
`[16, 32, 48, 64]`. Record shared and independent-per-tile residuals, required
rank, singular energy, and amortized work. Dense targets are explicitly oracle.

## Stage B: centered K/V latent family

For `M = [4, 8, 16]`, construct standardized features

\[
Z=[K,VP],
\]

where `P` is one fixed 16-dimensional orthogonal value sketch. Optimize one
anchor `u_0` and `r` latent queries for ranks `[16, 32, 48, 64]`:

\[
b_m=\operatorname{softmax}(Zu_m)^T V
-\operatorname{softmax}(Zu_0)^T V,
\qquad B=\operatorname{qr}([b_1,\ldots,b_r]).
\]

The same `B` is shared by every selected query tile; projection coefficients
remain target-exposed. Use exactly 80 Adam steps per rank stage, learning rate
0.03, gradient clipping 1.0, initialization standard deviation 0.5, and two
fixed restarts. No family, optimizer, rank, or tile-count search is allowed.

## Selection and test isolation

On validation, select the minimum-work centered candidate satisfying aggregate
relative L2 at most 1%, worst-head at most 2%, and at least 90% recovery of the
defect-energy reduction achieved by the shared adaptive basis at the same
`(M, rank)`. Tie by lower aggregate error, lower worst-head error, then smaller
`M` and rank. Freeze `(M, rank)` before fitting the same target-exposed centered
family once on test. Test may not choose a different tile count, rank, restart
budget, family, support, or optimizer.

## Cost model

For exact density `rho=0.35`, query tile `q=64`, output width `d=128`, feature
width `f=144`, shared rank `r`, and `M` tiles:

\[
C(M,r)=\rho+\frac{(r+1)f+rd}{M\,2qd}.
\]

This is an optimistic MAC fraction. It excludes coefficient prediction,
support/routing, normalization, memory traffic, QR, launch, and fusion costs.
No H200 latency or speedup claim is permitted.

## Outcome map

- `pass`: the frozen centered configuration reaches test 1% / 2%, recovers at
  least 90% of adaptive captured energy, and costs at most 55%.
- `cost-boundary`: it passes quality and recovery only above 55% and no greater
  than 65%.
- `transfer-boundary`: validation has an eligible centered candidate, but its
  frozen `(M, rank)` fails test quality or recovery.
- `generator-boundary`: a shared adaptive candidate passes at no more than 55%,
  but no centered validation candidate passes through 65%.
- `capacity-null`: no shared adaptive candidate passes at no more than 55%.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain their standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, pass, cost-boundary,
transfer-boundary, generator-boundary, then capacity-null.

## Guards and budget

- Every support has fixed registered density and finite positive partition.
- Shared and independent adaptive residuals must be finite; independent rank-r
  error cannot exceed shared rank-r error beyond `1e-6` relative tolerance.
- Centered weights sum to zero per basis direction; all basis/output values are
  finite, rank does not exceed 64, and raw singular ratios are recorded.
- Existing captures only; at most two H200-hours, one valid execution, and one
  pre-outcome engineering repair.
- Stop after one terminal outcome. No learned predictor, rollout, fused kernel,
  or measured speed claim is allowed inside this Gate.
