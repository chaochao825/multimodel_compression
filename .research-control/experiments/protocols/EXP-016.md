# EXP-016: Equal-cost distributed micro-anchor basis transfer

## Decision question

Can the same 64 dense-query-row budget that failed as one contiguous tile
recover the M8 rotating residual subspace when distributed across four regular
16-row micro-anchors from distinct query tiles?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-016`
- Candidate: `L-016`
- Gate: `G-016`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen scope

- Same Wan F81 Layer-14 step-9 conditional cell, two identities, split, 16
  stratified query tiles, M8 grouping, and 35% exact support as EXP-014/015.
- Split every 64-query tile into four contiguous 16-row candidates.
- Select exactly four candidates per M8 group, with at most one from any parent
  tile. The selected 64 rows execute dense and supply their exact residual.
- Test ranks 16, 32, 48, and 64. All remaining rows use target-exposed optimal
  projection coefficients; this Gate tests basis observability only.

## Registered selectors

- `single_q_medoid_tile`: all four blocks from the Q-medoid tile; EXP-015
  replication baseline and the only exception to the distinct-parent rule.
- `fixed_spread_4x16`: local parent tiles `[0,2,4,6]` with microblock indices
  `[0,1,2,3]`.
- `q_kcenter_4x16`: pooled-Q descriptors, initialized at their medoid, then
  greedy maximum minimum cosine distance, excluding used parent tiles.
- `q_pivoted_4x16`: centered pooled-Q descriptors with greedy residual-norm
  pivoting, excluding used parent tiles.
- `oracle_greedy_4x16`: target-exposed forward selection minimizing total
  post-projection residual after each added microblock, excluding used parent
  tiles. It is a diagnostic ceiling, not a deployable selector.

All selectors are shared by heads. Legal selectors never read V, dense output,
defect, projection coefficients, or held-out metrics.

## Metrics and cost

Selected rows have zero residual. For all unselected rows record aggregate and
per-head output relative L2, the adaptive rank-r floor on those same rows, and
the basis-inflation ratio.

With `rho=0.35`, 64 dense rows among `8*64` group rows, rank `r`, and key length
`N`, optimistic work is

\[
rho + (1-rho)/8 + r/N.
\]

Support selection, microblock gather, SVD, projection-coefficient generation,
normalization, memory, launch, and fusion are omitted and must be disclosed.

## Selection and outcome map

Validation selects the lowest aggregate-error legal selector/rank satisfying
1% / 2% and at most 50% work; tie by worst-head error, selector name, then
rank. Freeze selector and rank for one test execution.

- `strong-pass`: frozen legal test reaches 0.5% / 1%.
- `pass`: frozen legal test reaches 1% / 2% but not the strong Gate.
- `transfer-boundary`: a legal validation candidate passes but frozen test
  fails 1% / 2%.
- `selector-boundary`: oracle-greedy rank 64 passes validation, but no legal
  selector passes.
- `distributed-null`: even oracle-greedy rank 64 fails validation.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, strong-pass, pass,
transfer-boundary, selector-boundary, then distributed-null.

## Guards and budget

- Exactly 64 selected rows per group; legal distributed methods use four
  distinct parent tiles and all methods are shared by heads.
- Selected rows are exact; unselected target defects are evaluation/projection
  only. Legal selector APIs cannot accept target tensors.
- Load the validation capture first. The test capture may be opened only after a
  legal validation candidate passes all registered quality and work gates.
- Sampled-basis projection cannot beat adaptive SVD on the same unselected rows
  beyond `1e-6` relative tolerance.
- Existing captures only; at most one H200-hour, one valid execution, and one
  pre-outcome engineering repair. The repair was used before formal execution
  to enforce staged test-capture loading; it did not change selectors, ranks,
  geometry, metrics, costs, gates, or any numerical computation.
- No coefficient predictor, learned adapter, rollout, kernel, or speed claim.
