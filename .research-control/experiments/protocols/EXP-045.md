# EXP-045: Current-input denoising observability Gate

- Status: complete
- Claim: C-024
- Candidate: L-024
- Gate: G-024
- Kind: prospective exploratory Gate

## Scientific scope

This experiment tests cross-sampler-step observability of a frozen Wan block
residual. It does not model physical time between adjacent generated video
segments and does not test an autoregressive long-video memory.

## Frozen model and cells

- Frozen Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B checkpoint and unchanged 20-step UniPC sampler.
- F17 at 480x832, latent token grid 5x30x52, CFG scale 5.0.
- Contiguous blocks 20--29, target sampler steps 4 and 6, both CFG branches.
- Current block input `h`, exact block residual `r`, all six current AdaLN
  modulation fields, and a fixed-row Q/K sketch are diagnostic observables.
- The Q/K sketch uses 8 evenly spaced output rows from each projection and costs
  `2 * N * D * 8` MACs per observed cell. Full Q/K projection is prohibited.

## Prospective identity boundary

- Twelve new prompt-seed identities are frozen before capture.
- `calibration`: identities 0--3; implementation and fixed hyperparameters only.
- `selection`: identities 4--7; no-training Gate and optional router selection.
- `final`: identities 8--11; unopened until a router is frozen.
- EXP-004 prompts and endpoints are audit context only and cannot train, select,
  or tune any EXP-045 method.

## Causal information contract

At target step `k` and open-loop horizon `H`, step `k-H` is the last exact
anchor. The predictor may observe `h` and conditioning through step `k`, but may
observe exact `r` only through the anchor. Intermediate predicted residuals do
not update secants, regressions, masks, or expert coefficients.

All transport operators are zero-padded nonperiodic shifts on the 5x30x52 token
lattice. No operator may permute the hidden-channel axis based on its index.

## Frozen no-training methods

1. Matched scalar AR(2), with one pair of coefficients per layer, CFG branch,
   and sampler transition fitted only from the four calibration identities.
   Fixed Taylor extrapolation and sample-local online AR(2) are reported as
   controls but cannot replace the frozen matched baseline after seeing selection.
2. Causal channel-diagonal regression from exact history using previous residual
   and current block-input drift.
3. Sample-local multisecant Broyden with 2 or 4 exact secants.
4. Two or four causal nonperiodic transport experts selected and fit only from
   exact historical pairs, plus current-input innovation.
5. Conditional DPLR rank 8/16 as a diagnostic control, with all fitting restricted
   to exact historical pairs.
6. A target-visible 75-shift token-LS oracle as a ceiling only.

The primary structured candidate is

`r_hat_k = D(z_k) r_{k-1} + sum_m g_m(z_k) P_m r_{k-1} + B_k(h_k-h_{k-1})`,

where `P_m` acts on spacetime token positions.

## Metrics

- Full-tensor residual relative L2 per identity, layer, step, branch, method, and
  horizon 1/2/3.
- Aggregate and worst-identity risk, branch harm, and per-layer pass count.
- Oracle recovery fraction
  `(R_AR2 - R_method) / (R_AR2 - R_oracle)`.
- Predictor, observable, Q/K sketch, and transport arithmetic separately.
- Peak host/GPU memory and measured diagnostic overhead; no end-to-end speed claim.

## G-024

The no-training Gate passes only if one causal method:

- reduces horizon-1 risk by at least 2x versus matched AR(2) in at least 6 of 10
  layers at both target steps on the selection split;
- recovers at least 50% of the positive oracle gap at both target steps;
- harms neither CFG branch by more than 10% versus matched AR(2);
- is no worse than matched AR(2) in aggregate at horizons 2 and 3; and
- has complete accounting for every observable and predictor operation.

## Resource and artifact policy

- One locked H200, at most four GPU-hours for the no-training Gate.
- Full tensors remain in host memory for one identity and are released after the
  preregistered evaluator completes. Only metrics, manifests, limited debug rows,
  and plots are persisted because the remote root has insufficient safe capacity
  for full trajectories.
- No VAE decode, approximate rollout, checkpoint mutation, full-model training,
  or kernel claim is permitted.

## Stop rule and outcome

Stop on implementation-equivalence failure, data-boundary violation, foreign GPU
overlap, resource exhaustion, or one valid G-024 decision. If G-024 fails, close
L-024 as a bounded observability null. If it passes, freeze one width-32 router on
calibration, select it on selection, and only then open the final identities.

## Outcome

G-024 failed on 2026-08-20. DPLR-16 passed 3/10 layers at each target step but
lacked registered open-loop evidence; the strongest complete method, Broyden-2,
passed 2/10 layers and reached a 1.530 worst open-loop risk ratio. The final split
remained unopened and no router, rollout, or kernel stage was authorized. See
`.research-control/results/RESULT-EXP-045.md`.
