# EXP-039: Anchor leave-one-out risk-observability screen

## Decision question

Do exact progressive micro-anchor residuals contain a target-free self-error
signal that predicts unseen-row basis risk strongly enough to justify collecting
fresh identities for a prospective online certificate?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-018`
- Candidate: `L-018`
- Gate: `G-018`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory mechanism screen

## Frozen split and access

- Same Wan F81 Layer-14 step-9 conditional cell, 16 query tiles, M8 groups,
  35% target-exposed support, and Q-k-center progressive blocks as EXP-038.
- Cross-fit calibration records: evaluate `s00` with prior built only from `s01`;
  evaluate `s01` with prior built only from `s00`.
- Identity-held-out screen: evaluate `s02` once with prior built from `s00+s01`.
- `s03` tensor contents and all EXP-038 test labels are prohibited.
- Non-anchor target projections are labels only. LOAO features may use only Q,
  selected exact anchor defects/references, calibration priors, and registered
  geometry.

## Frozen LOAO feature

For each identity, candidate, stage, M8 group, and head, rotate over selected
16-row anchor blocks. Build the innovation basis from all other selected blocks,
using the largest feasible registered innovation rank, project the held-out
anchor defect, and accumulate squared residual and exact reference energy.

The primary score is:

`sqrt(sum held-out residual_sq / sum held-out anchor reference_sq)`.

Record the rank deficit caused by leave-one-block-out. Do not correct the score
using unselected rows. Actual unselected-row relative L2 is the diagnostic label.

## Frozen calibration and metrics

- Report pooled Spearman correlation on `s02` between LOAO score and actual
  unseen-row output error over all 108 candidate-stage-head records.
- Report top-quartile high-risk recall by selecting the 27 largest LOAO scores
  and measuring recall of the 27 largest actual errors.
- For each of the nine candidate-stage actions, fit on cross-fit `s00+s01` a
  conservative multiplier equal to the maximum finite ratio
  `actual_error / max(loao_score, 1e-8)`.
- On `s02`, a record is certified when multiplier times LOAO score is at most
  2%. Report false-safe count and certified coverage.
- Report stage-delta Spearman as a secondary diagnostic only.

## Outcome map

- `strong-signal`: Spearman at least 0.75, top-quartile recall at least 0.90,
  zero false-safe records, and certified coverage at least 50%.
- `signal`: Spearman at least 0.60, top-quartile recall at least 0.75, zero
  false-safe records, and certified coverage at least 25%.
- `observability-null`: any registered signal gate fails.
- `invalid` and `engineering-failure` retain standard meanings.

Outcome precedence is invalid, engineering-failure, strong-signal, signal, then
observability-null.
