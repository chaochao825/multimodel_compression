# EXP-005: CMAQ block-control-variate capacity and transfer probe

## Decision question

After a hardware-cheap hierarchy of full-support block-centroid estimates, do
exact block corrections have sufficiently concentrated and transferable variance for
regular-block control-variate sampling to meet the strict local AV gates at no
more than 37.5% draw work?

## Claim and candidate

- Claim: `C-005`
- Candidate: `L-005`
- Gate: `G-005`
- Lane: explore
- Kind: exploratory

## Frozen data and split

- Model/trajectory: existing Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B F81, 20-step UniPC, CFG 5.0
  post-RoPE Q/K/V replays.
- Cell: Layer 14, sampling step 9, conditional branch.
- Query scope: three deterministic stratified 64-query tiles per capture.
- Calibration: `s00_p00_seed20260740`, `s01_p01_seed20260740`.
- Held-out: `s02_p00_seed20260741`, `s03_p01_seed20260741`.
- Key blocks: contiguous 64-token blocks, padding only in the final block.
- Draw densities: 0.125, 0.25, 0.375, and 0.50 of the block count.
- Monte Carlo repetitions and seeds are fixed in the config before outcomes.

## Frozen estimators

For each query row and block, use one common score shift and compute exact
unnormalized numerator/denominator `(N_B, Z_B)`. Compare global-centroid,
256-token parent-centroid, and 64-token child-centroid control variates. The
registered sampler uses the child-centroid baseline; unlike a full K/V
cross-covariance term, it preserves a plausible sub-dense arithmetic path.
Corrections are:

`delta_N_B = N_B - coarse_N_B`,
`delta_Z_B = Z_B - coarse_Z_B`.

Compare these methods:

1. global-, parent-, and child-centroid full-support baselines;
2. oracle top-k exact replacement by current joint correction norm;
3. calibration-frozen top-k exact replacement;
4. moment/value-proxy top-k exact replacement without dense attention;
5. uniform with-replacement correction sampling after 12.5% proxy-selected
   exact spikes;
6. moment/value-proxy PPS sampling after the same exact spikes;
5. oracle PPS sampling proportional to current joint correction norm;
6. calibration-frozen PPS using the calibration-mean head/tile/block proposal.

The moment/value proxy uses only pooled block K/V means, centered RMS, maximum
V norm, and the current query tile. It is a deployment-shaped diagnostic, not a
learned router. All PPS variants reserve 12.5% block work for exact spikes and
spend only the remaining registered budget on with-replacement correction
draws.

The stochastic estimator is the fixed-draw Hansen-Hurwitz estimator around the
coarse full-support control variate. It reports draw density and unique executed
block density separately. Oracle methods are target-leaking capacity ceilings.

## Primary endpoints

- aggregate and worst-record output relative L2;
- median and P95 across frozen Monte Carlo repetitions; P95 is used for gates;
- invalid/non-positive denominator rate and non-finite count;
- empirical coverage and certified-row rate for a 99% joint numerator /
  denominator delta-method radius;
- top-1/5/10/25% joint correction energy, normalized effective support, and
  calibration-to-held-out proposal overlap;
- draw density, unique density, and ideal arithmetic proxy only.

No arithmetic proxy is an H200 timing claim.

## Outcome mapping

- `pass`: at or below 37.5% draw density, oracle reaches aggregate/worst
  `<=0.5%/1%`, calibration-frozen PPS reaches `<=1%/2%`, all denominators are
  positive and finite, and frozen PPS reduces squared aggregate error by at
  least 25% versus the same-density calibration-frozen top-k baseline. Open only
  a fresh proposal/certificate Gate.
- `boundary`: oracle passes `0.5%/1%` by 37.5%, but the frozen proposal misses
  `1%/2%` or the 25% relative-improvement guard. Stop train-free CMAQ; a learned
  proposal requires another decision.
- `null`: oracle cannot reach `0.5%/1%` by 50%, or control-variate sampling does
  not improve the deterministic baseline. Close CMAQ.
- `adverse`: either deployable frozen/moment exact-plus-sampling estimator produces a
  non-positive denominator, non-finite output, or catastrophic error above
  10%. Coarse diagnostic baselines do not trigger this class. Close without
  tuning.
- `invalid`: capture/split/config mismatch, dense parity failure, stale output,
  test-dependent proposal construction, or reproducibility failure. Repair once
  before inspecting scientific outcomes.
- `engineering-failure`: the frozen implementation cannot complete within the
  resource cap despite one allowed repair. Preserve logs and close without a
  scientific conclusion.

## Budget and stop rule

One numerical pass over four existing captures, one frozen analysis, one
pre-outcome repair, and at most two H200 GPU-hours. No new model generation,
training, rollout, kernel, or speed claim. Stop after classifying G-005.

## Registered pre-outcome repair

The first attempt stopped on the first calibration tile before writing any
scientific metric: blockwise FP32 reduction differed from `softmax @ V` by
`1.656679e-5`, above an overly strict `2e-6` parity guard. The sole allowed
repair changed only this validation tolerance to `5e-5`; estimator equations,
data split, random seeds, densities, and outcome gates remain frozen.
