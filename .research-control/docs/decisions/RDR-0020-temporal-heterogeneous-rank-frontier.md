# RDR-0020: Authorize bounded temporal heterogeneous-rank frontier

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-12
- Decider: researcher through the explicit request to continue high-information
  exploration after valid negative results
- Supersedes: none

## Context

EXP-040 rejected a uniform rank-64 temporal marginal-defect basis at its strong
capacity gate, but exposed a narrow, previously untested mechanism. Adjacent-
step bases had mean projector overlap 0.877 with the current defect versus 0.646
for a same-step calibration basis, and reduced aggregate error by 51.3% relative
to that static control. The residual failure was concentrated in a few heads and
the rank-64/65 singular gap was nearly flat.

The validation identity `s02` is now exposed. It may be used only to select a
bounded per-head action table. The `s03` capture generated by EXP-040 was not
loaded and remains the sole prospective transfer test.

## Decision

Authorize one mechanism Gate at Wan F81 Layer 14, conditional branch, targets
8/9/10. Each head selects one action shared across targets and M8 groups from
previous-step defect ranks 32/48/64/80/96 or exact dense fallback. At most two
heads may fall back to dense and mean local optimistic work must remain at most
50%. The selected table is frozen before `s03` is loaded.

Every non-dense action is compared with the same-rank same-step calibration
basis under the identical 35% target-exposed exact support. Coefficients remain
target projections. Current-anchor innovation and chordal extrapolation are not
reopened because EXP-040 already showed that they worsen the nearest-previous
basis.

## Consequences

- A frozen-test pass at 1% aggregate, 2% worst head-step, at least 20% relative
  improvement over matched static bases, two-thirds passing target steps, and
  at most 50% mean optimistic work authorizes only a target-free coefficient
  and source-refresh amortization Gate.
- Validation or transfer failure parks train-free temporal marginal-defect
  transport on this screened cell. Rank 112/128, support search, innovation,
  rotation, and post-outcome threshold changes are prohibited.
- A pass remains a representation/transport result. Target-exposed support and
  coefficients, dense source refresh, omitted runtime costs, and absence of a
  rollout or fused kernel prohibit speed, deployment, or novelty claims.

## Resource boundary

- Reuse only the immutable EXP-040 capture index and payloads; collect no new
  tensor and do not load `s03` before validation passes.
- One valid H200 analysis execution, at most one H200-hour, and one pre-outcome
  engineering repair.

## Revisit condition

Only a different learned sparse-linear function class or a materially different
hardware cost model may revive the line after a valid null.

## Closure

Closed as `strong-temporal-frontier`. The validation-frozen head table reached
the staged `s03` at 0.486% / 0.874%, improved 65.8% over matched static, passed
all targets, and used 46.02% local optimistic work. This opens only a separate
target-free coefficient and refresh-amortization Gate; no speed claim follows.
