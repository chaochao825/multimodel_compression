# Program Roadmap

Updated: 2026-08-12

## Horizon 0: preserve the closed evidence

- Preserve C-000 through C-020, RDR-0000 through RDR-0020, all protocols,
  manifests, raw artifacts, nulls, boundaries, and narrow positive witnesses.
- Keep fixed BCM/BCCB/Butterfly, local sparse attention, static low-rank,
  low-precision correction, cache, and old temporal prediction outside the
  active portfolio.

Exit gate: RDR-0021 is accepted and the prior evidence is unchanged.

## Horizon 1: freeze the curvature probe

- PLAN-044 completed without GPU execution or tensor-payload access.
- Immutable trajectories were audited and found insufficient: no per-step state
  is paired with guided model velocity, and the available F81 captures use a
  history-dependent UniPC solver.
- Exact finite-interval curvature, observable and oracle warps, equal-budget
  controls, evaluator semantics, cost model, identities, and stop mappings are
  frozen in the proposed EXP-042/G-021 protocol.
- EXP-042/G-021 awaits explicit acceptance; no execution is authorized.

Exit gate: one prospective protocol can distinguish coordinate benefit,
structure benefit, observability failure, leakage, and generic flow-map capacity.

## Horizon 2: resolve representation and transfer

- Compare raw-noisy, clean-prediction, and current-observable motion-aligned
  coordinates under one fixed nonperiodic structure and equal payload.
- Keep target-exposed capacity separate from deployable frozen transfer.
- Evaluate risk-weighted curvature, 4-NFE endpoint, composition, and cost.
- Park L-021 on any registered capacity, alignment-gain, transfer, or cost miss.

Exit gate: C-021 is supported, narrowed, refuted, or invalid under one frozen
decision-bearing result. No component growth is an allowed outcome.

## Horizon 3: bounded tiny-adapter adaptation

- Open only after Horizon 2 passes.
- Freeze Wan and train only the motion-coordinate and curvature adapter under a
  separately accepted low-step, low-parameter, multi-interval protocol.
- Compare against Euler, equal-budget unstructured adapter, DMF/TMD-style flow
  map adaptation, and teacher sampling without changing evaluation identities.

Exit gate: a 4-NFE adapter passes latent trajectory and composition guards on
untouched identities within 10% per-NFE adapter arithmetic.

## Horizon 4: rollout and systems validation

- Open only after Horizon 3 passes.
- Run multi-prompt, multi-seed complete videos and paired teacher-relative
  quality, VBench, motion/physics metrics, and H200 wall-clock timing.
- Fuse only the already-supported coordinate/curvature path; do not add local
  attention, cache, sparse, quantization, or fallback claims without a new RDR.

Exit gate: verified quality and H200 acceleration support a paper claim, or the
line closes with a bounded explanation of representation, observability,
adaptation, rollout, or systems failure.
